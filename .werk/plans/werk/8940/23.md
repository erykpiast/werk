## What to build

Each project has one conversation at a time, with its own agent process. The conversation records its home card: the card that was open when the conversation started. The home card is its label in the chat header row and its first-turn context. It is a label, not a fence: the open-card line still names whatever card is open. The clear-context action starts a fresh conversation, and the fresh conversation takes the card open at that moment as its home card. The home card is saved beside the conversation and comes back after a server restart.

Opening the chat pane, also after a reload, scrolls the chat log to the newest message. While the pane stays open, the log follows new messages only when the person is near the bottom, so a person who scrolled up to read is not pulled down.

A child that exits without werk stopping it marks the conversation dead until the next child starts: the chat header row shows the agent as dead, the pane shows the whole error, and the open asks and the background count are dropped. A deliberate stop (a model switch, a clear, the server shutting down) is not a death, and a crash that werk retries is not a death until the retry also fails or the failure is one that no retry fixes.

While a background agent of the conversation runs, a line under the chat log says so, and the conversation counts as running. The count is the length of the last full list of background tasks the agent sent; it becomes zero when the child exits. A last agent line of the form `Next: say "<phrase>"` adds a button with that phrase under the message; a click sends it as a normal message with the open card and leaves the composer draft alone. The next message removes every such button.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The first turn of a new conversation carries its home card, and the chat header row names it.
- [ ] Clear context starts a fresh conversation whose home card is the card open at that moment.
- [ ] After a server restart the conversation shows the same home card.
- [ ] Opening the pane on a long conversation shows its newest message; a new message does not scroll a person who scrolled up.
- [ ] A fake that exits with code 1 marks the conversation dead and drops its open ask; the next message starts a child and clears the mark.
- [ ] A model switch shows no dead mark.
- [ ] A background task list of one keeps the conversation running after its turn ends, with the line shown; an empty list removes the line.
- [ ] A `Next: say "merge it"` line shows a button that sends "merge it" and keeps the composer draft.

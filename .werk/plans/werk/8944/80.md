## What to build

On a card page, the first section after the hero is "Kill criteria", above Options and the link lists. It has one row per recorded criterion, in two lines. Line one holds the state chip at the left edge (in v0 every criterion reads "recorded" with the neutral border, because v0 watches no criterion and so has no fired or unmonitored state), the trigger chip, and on the right a link to the rejected option the criterion falls back to, when it names one. Line two states the criterion in plain words: for a metric, its source, metric, threshold, window and minimum sample; for the other forms, the manual test, the no-metric reason, or "revisit at any time".

A decided card with no criterion shows the section with the unchallenged ring and the words that no criterion is recorded. A card with no criteria that is not decided shows no section. The hero carries no kill content. The section is server-rendered and never hydrates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A card with a metric criterion shows the section first after the hero, with source, threshold and window on line two.
- [ ] A card with a no-metric criterion shows its reason; one with "revisit at any time" says so.
- [ ] A criterion that names a rejected option links to that option.
- [ ] A decided card with no criterion shows the ring and the missing-criterion line.
- [ ] An open card with no criterion shows no section.

## What to build

`/board` shows the project's board from the board fold: columns blocked, ready, building, merging and done, each at least 200 pixels wide, sharing extra width, with a sideways scrollbar on a narrow screen. A ticket card shows its title cut to two lines with an ellipsis and a tooltip for the full title, a retry count when it retried more than once, and a count of open blockers. A building ticket whose session runs shows a pulsing dot pinned to its top-left corner; only the first title line moves aside. A session runs only while the board log has its start and no end and its named process is alive, so a builder killed from a terminal stops pulsing at once. A done ticket shows a red badge for a red held-out step linking to its follow-up, and a grey badge for a step not run.

Clicking a card opens a drawer under the board: a header with the id, the running dot and the controls, then the full title; the slice on the left rendered like a card body; a sidebar on the right with cited cards (each opening the map), blockers, retries, checks, the stop reason, and a Decisions section that lists the cards its builds proposed with proposal or accepted, or "No new card" with the cards followed; a ticket no build has started shows no Decisions section. For a building ticket, a fixed-height live preview above the slice shows one summarised line per event of its session file, overseer lines included, each with its full time; it goes when the ticket leaves building. A line above the columns shows G2 per subtree, waiting or passed. The board redraws on the live channel.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fixture board log shows each ticket in its column.
- [ ] A ticket with a long title shows two lines and an ellipsis; the drawer shows it in full.
- [ ] A building ticket pulses while its process lives and stops when the process is killed, with no new event.
- [ ] The drawer of a building ticket shows its live log lines; a ready ticket's drawer shows none.
- [ ] A cited card id in the sidebar opens that card on the map.
- [ ] The Decisions section shows a proposed card, and "No new card" with the followed cards for a build that recorded them.
- [ ] At a narrow width the board scrolls sideways and columns keep their width.

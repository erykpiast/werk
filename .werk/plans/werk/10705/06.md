## What to build

The link verb adds one typed link between two nodes: refines, depends_on, supported_by or alternative_to. Unlink removes one; the log keeps it. A depends_on link needs a reason, what the first card takes from the second, and linking the same pair again replaces the reason. A depends_on link to an ancestor or a descendant is refused with the reason. A move that turns an existing link into such a link removes that link. An option links to its card by alternative_to only.

A link on a ghost works: the edge is held, drawn on the ghost, and goes live by itself once both ends are real.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A depends_on link without a reason exits non-zero and appends nothing.
- [ ] A depends_on link from a card to its parent or grandparent is refused, and the message says why.
- [ ] Linking the same pair again replaces the shown reason.
- [ ] A link from an option with a type other than alternative_to is refused.
- [ ] A link on a ghost shows as held in show, and the card's live edges do not include it.
- [ ] The link rules are on the pure-file list and pass the coverage gate.

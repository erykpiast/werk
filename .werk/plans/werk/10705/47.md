## What to build

A red held-out result after a merge shows as a red badge on the done ticket whose merge it followed, on its card and in its drawer metadata, linking to the follow-up ticket; the badge stays after the follow-up goes green. A step that did not run shows as a grey badge instead. The follow-up ticket itself appears in ready. No column is added.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A red held-out result in the board log shows a red badge on the merged ticket that links to its follow-up.
- [ ] A not-run result shows a grey badge.
- [ ] The board still has exactly five columns.

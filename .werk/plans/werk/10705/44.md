## What to build

The board view, at its own address, draws the board fold in five columns: blocked, ready, building, merging, done. Each column is at least 200 pixels wide and grows to share the rest; a narrow pane scrolls the board sideways. A ticket card shows its title cut to two lines with an ellipsis and the full title on hover, a retry count after more than one try, and a count of open blockers. A building ticket whose session is running by the board log and a live process has a pulsing dot pinned in the top-left corner.

A click opens a drawer beneath the board. The header shows the id, the running dot and the controls, then the full title. The main column shows the ticket text; the sidebar shows cites (each opens its card on the map), blockers, retries, checks, stories and touches, and a Decisions section: each card the ticket's builds proposed and whether it is accepted, or "No new card" with the followed cards. For a building ticket, a fixed-height live log above the text shows one summarised line per stream event with its full time, read from the session log; it goes when the ticket leaves building. The sidebar and the log each have a toggle that is kept across redraws and loads. The board redraws on each board log change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fixture board log shows each ticket in its column, and a move from the command line redraws the board.
- [ ] A long title shows two lines and an ellipsis, and the drawer shows it in full.
- [ ] A building ticket pulses only while its session process is alive.
- [ ] The drawer of a building ticket shows the live log of its session.
- [ ] A ticket whose build proposed a card lists that card in Decisions.
- [ ] At 600 pixels wide, the board scrolls sideways and no column is under 200 pixels.

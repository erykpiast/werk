## What to build

A plan over a subtree that already has a spec updates that spec and rewrites its open tickets, ready or blocked, under their own ids; new tickets take numbers after the highest; an open ticket that the new spec no longer needs is archived with that reason. Tickets in building, merging or done stay as they are. The board never holds two ticket sets for one subtree.

When a plan puts a ticket on the board, the board records the version of each card it cites. A watcher reads each change to the decision log and finds every open ticket that cites the changed card. A change of title, why, body, status, removal, a stale mark or a depends_on edge moves that ticket to blocked with a reason that names the card and the kind of change. A note does not count. A card added under a planned subtree runs the cards-cited check again and reports the result without blocking a ticket. A later plan that keeps the ticket clears the mark.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A second plan over the same subtree changes ticket 02 in place, adds ticket 04, and archives an open ticket the new spec drops.
- [ ] A done ticket is unchanged by a second plan.
- [ ] Reopening a card that a ready ticket cites moves the ticket to blocked with the card in the reason.
- [ ] A note on a cited card blocks nothing.
- [ ] A building ticket stays in building when its cited card changes.

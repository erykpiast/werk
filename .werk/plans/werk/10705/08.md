## What to build

The status verb moves a card to decided only after the contract check passes four facts: who is set; a basis exists (a decided, not stale depends_on target or parent) or a basis waiver with a reason; a weighed option when who is you or not set, or an option waiver with a reason; and a kill criterion on record. A refusal lists every missing fact, in that order, each with the exact command that fixes it and the card id filled in.

The edit verb sets who, records the basis waiver and the unchallenged statement, each with a non-empty reason. The kill verb records one criterion: a metric with source, threshold, window and sample, a manual test, a stated reason for no metric, or revisit at any time. The check runs the same on a ghost against its held edges, so a ghost can be proposed already decided and commit as one unit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A bare card with no who is refused once, listing who, basis, option and kill criterion with their fix commands.
- [ ] A card with who me needs no option.
- [ ] A waiver with an empty reason is refused, and show prints a stored waiver reason.
- [ ] A card whose basis is open is refused and names that basis.
- [ ] A ghost with held basis, option and criterion becomes decided and stays a ghost until accepted.
- [ ] The contract rules pass the coverage gate.

## What to build

The find verb lists real open and proto cards in the refines subtree of a card, breadth first, the card itself included, or across every tree when no card is named. It leaves out options, sources, excerpts, ghosts, removed cards, and every card with a paused, stale or postponed mark. A card reopened as rejected is listed.

The search verb with no term prints the index: one line per decided card with its id and title. With terms, a card matches when every case-insensitive term occurs in its title, why, body or the why-not of one of its options; the most hits come first. A status filter and a subtree filter narrow the result. A ghost or a removed card never matches.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Find on a subtree lists an open and a proto card and leaves out a decided, a paused, a stale and a postponed one.
- [ ] Search with no term prints one line per decided card and nothing else.
- [ ] Search with two terms returns only cards that hold both, the one with more hits first.
- [ ] A term that occurs only in an option's why-not finds the card.

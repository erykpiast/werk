## What to build

No view edits a drawn card in place: every control appends an event through the server, which carries the token, and the map redraws from the log. A card with a pending proposal shows a ✓ in its top-right actions; a click appends the same accept event as the command line with the person as actor. Discard has no button.

A pending edit renders as a diff inside the card: added text has a blue highlight, removed text a blue highlight and a strikethrough, in the title, the why and the body; lists pair old and new items one by one so one changed item is marked alone; a block that changes kind shows the old removed and the new added. A new ghost card shows a blue border and background with the badge "proposed"; an edit shows a blue border only. A proposed removal is drawn in red.

The ✕ in the card view asks once and names how many cards leave. For a card with children it offers three answers: remove the subtree, remove the card and move its children to the named parent, or cancel. The diff renderer is a pure module on the coverage list.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Clicking ✓ on a ghost makes it real, and the page redraws without a reload.
- [ ] A pending body edit shows the changed words highlighted and nothing else marked.
- [ ] One added item in a long list marks only that item.
- [ ] The ✕ on a card with two children offers subtree removal and reparent, each with its count.
- [ ] A write request from the page without the token is refused.

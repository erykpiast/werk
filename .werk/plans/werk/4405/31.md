## What to build

After the checks of a pass are green, a grader agent with the role grader reads the diff. It scores 1 to 5, with what is wrong and where, one delivery line per acceptance criterion, one contradiction line per decided card in the ticket's depends_on closure (cited or not), one scope line that fails when the diff builds a behaviour no criterion or story asks for, and the obligation record of the pass. A 3 passes. A card the grader leaves out scores 0. A part of a card the diff does not build yet passes its contradiction line.

On a passing grade, five reviewers run at once, one per area (design, maintainability, tests, security and dependencies, performance), each with the role `reviewer-<area>` and the diff only. Each finding has a severity, file, line, impact and fix. A CRITICAL or HIGH finding fails the pass; MEDIUM and LOW are recorded and do not block; an unknown severity counts as MEDIUM. The grader's gaps and the blocking findings go into the next pass's brief. A broken or silent grader or reviewer fails the pass as a failed step.

Every judge starts with a fixed tool list of file reads, text search, file find and the git operations that show a diff, the history and one commit. It cannot ask for another tool: every other call is refused at once and no ask reaches the overseer.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fake grader scoring one criterion 2 fails the pass and the next brief names that criterion.
- [ ] A fake reviewer-security with one HIGH finding fails the pass; the same finding as LOW does not.
- [ ] A grader reply that omits a closure card fails the pass.
- [ ] Each judge launch is recorded with its role and the read-only tool list.
- [ ] A grader that exits without a verdict fails the pass as a broken step.

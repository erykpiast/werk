## What to build

An agent runs `werk status <id> decided`. The contract check reads the folded map and tests four facts: who is set; a basis exists (a decided, not stale depends_on target or refines parent, or a written basis waiver); when who is you or not set, an alternative_to option exists or a written option waiver; a kill criterion is on record. A refused decide lists every missing fact in that order, each with the exact command that fixes it, the card id filled in. A card whose basis is open waits: the command fails and names that basis.

`werk edit <id>` sets `--who`, records a basis waiver or an option waiver; each waiver needs a written reason, and an empty reason is refused. `werk kill <id>` records one kill criterion per call: a metric with source, threshold and window, a manual test, a no-metric reason, or revisit at any time. Waivers and criteria print wherever the card prints. On a ghost, the check runs against its held edges, so a card can be proposed already decided.

The amend scope rule: a change to the title, why, body, a depends_on link or the parent of a decided card requires all four facts after the change; any other change is refused only when it removes a fact the card had, so a missing fact can always be added. The check is a pure module on the coverage list.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Deciding a bare card is refused once, naming who, basis, option and kill criterion with their fix commands.
- [ ] A card with who me needs no option; a card with who you or no who does.
- [ ] A waiver with an empty reason is refused; a waiver with a reason prints on the card.
- [ ] A card on an open basis is refused and the message names the basis.
- [ ] Removing the last option of a decided card is refused; recording a kill criterion on an old decided card without one is allowed.
- [ ] A ghost with held edges, options and a kill criterion can be set decided and commits decided on accept.

## What to build

An agent runs `werk propose <id>` with a new title, why, or a list of ops on the body. An op is elaborated (old and new), new (new) or removed (old). Every block of the current body must be the old of an op, else the command fails and prints the blocks at risk. An old that does not quote a real block is shown as removed plus new and never trusted. The proposal is staged: the card shows a pending change, the frozen fields stay as they were.

`werk accept <id> --actor you` applies the proposal. On a decided card the accept is an amend: a reopen and a decide in one step, so the map never shows the card open, and the amend runs the contract facts on the new version. `werk discard <id>` drops the pending proposal. An open card changes through a proposal too and marks nothing. Applying ops to a body is a pure module on the coverage list.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A proposal that leaves a body block unnamed is refused and lists that block.
- [ ] A quoted old that is not a real block shows as removed plus new.
- [ ] Accepting a proposal on a decided card leaves it decided with the new words, and records one amend.
- [ ] An amend that removes a required fact is refused.
- [ ] Discard drops the pending change and leaves the card as it was.

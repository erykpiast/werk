## What to build

Each conversation has its own agent process and a home card: the card that was open when it started, used as its tab label and its first-turn context. The home card is a label, not a fence. A tab strip below the composer shows one chip per conversation; a person starts a new conversation on the open card and switches between them. Asks, the dead-child bar and Always-allow rules stay with their own conversation. Switching to a tab scrolls its chat log to the newest message; while a tab stays open the log follows new messages only when the person is near the bottom.

A child that exits without werk stopping it marks its tab dead until the tab's next child starts: its chip blinks red, its asks and background agents are dropped, its own pane shows the error, and every other pane's dead-child bar shows one line naming the dead tab and its error. A pending ask blinks the chip yellow. A deliberate stop (a model switch, a clear, the server shutting down) is not a death.

While a background agent of a tab runs, its pane shows a line under the chat log, and the tab counts as running; the count is the length of the last full list of background tasks the agent sent, and becomes zero when the child exits. A last agent line of the form `Next: say "<phrase>"` adds a button with that phrase; a click sends it as a normal message, and the next message removes all such buttons in that tab.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two tabs with different home cards each stream their own replies, and an ask in one does not show in the other.
- [ ] Switching to a tab shows its newest message.
- [ ] A fake that exits with code 1 marks its chip red and puts a line in the other tab's pane; the next message clears both.
- [ ] A background task list of one keeps the tab running after its turn ends; an empty list makes it idle.
- [ ] A `Next: say "merge it"` line shows a button that sends "merge it".

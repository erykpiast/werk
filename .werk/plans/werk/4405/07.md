## What to build

The fold derives the stale, paused, postponed and waiting marks and their traces, so every rule and view reads them from one place. A reopen with the reason invalidated (the default) or rejected, and an amend, mark the direct depends_on dependents and the direct refines children stale. A reopen with the reason postponed pauses every card reached over refines children and depends_on dependents that existed at that moment, at any distance; the postponed card itself carries the postponed mark and stays open. When a postponement and an invalidation reach one card, stale wins. A link made after a change carries no mark from it. A card whose basis is stale or open waits and is not stale. A paused card still counts as a basis. Deciding a postponed card again clears the paused marks it caused, and only those.

Each mark carries its trace: the origin card, the kind (invalidated, rejected, postponed or amended), the date, the reopen text, the link and its reason; a paused trace also names each card on the path, nearest to the origin first, and the last link type.

`werk reopen <id> --reason --why` reopens a decided card. `werk rederive <id>` ends a review: a confirm clears the card's marks and requires the basis to be decided and every outgoing link reason confirmed, printing the fix commands otherwise; `--status decided` runs the contract check. An amend at the end of a review goes through a proposal and marks the next hop with the trace amended. `werk stale [<id>] [--kind]` lists marked cards in fix order, a basis before its dependent, each with its trace line, and exits 0 with a message when nothing is marked. `werk show` prints each mark's trace and, for a flagged depends_on row, the reopen reason, date and text.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reopening A marks only its direct dependents and direct children stale; a card two hops away is unmarked.
- [ ] Postponing P pauses a card three hops downstream, and P itself shows postponed while still open.
- [ ] Deciding P again clears those paused marks and leaves a pause from another card in place.
- [ ] A card linked after the reopen carries no mark; a card linked to an open basis waits.
- [ ] A confirm is refused while a link reason is unconfirmed, and the message gives the command.
- [ ] `werk stale` prints a basis before the card that depends on it, each with origin, reason and date.
- [ ] A paused trace names every card on its path in order.
- [ ] The mark derivation is a pure module on the coverage list and passes the gate.

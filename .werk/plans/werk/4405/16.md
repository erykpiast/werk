## What to build

Every card has one page at `/map/<id>`, with the detail on top and the sub-cards below; the root view is the page of the card #root. The hero box has a tag row (id that copies itself on click, kind, status, each mark as a word, who label), a title that shrinks with depth, the full why and body rendered with live links, the rollup bar of decided, open and proto widths and a line "this card and everything below it, N cards" with a count per status. Under the hero: a "Why this card is stale" section with each mark's trace, a "Why a dependency is flagged" section, then Options, Evidence, Say (excerpts with speaker and words), Depends on and Depend on this card, each link row showing `#id` in a lighter colour, the title and the link's reason.

Sub-cards are tiles in a grid: real sub-cards in log order, then ghosts. A tile is one link to its card, with the title, the why line rendered inline with no live links, and one meta row (id, cards below, evidence count, who label with the unchallenged ring, planning mark), and its rollup bar when it has children. The heading gives the count and the ghost count. A card is drawn the same way in tile and hero: the left edge carries the status colour; stale tints the box red; postponed draws grey diagonal stripes and a grey postponed tag; a ghost has a blue border and background. A postponed card keeps its status width in a parent's bar, hatched grey. Static parts render on the server; only interactive parts hydrate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `/map` opens the root card page with its sub-card tiles.
- [ ] A card with an option, a source and an excerpt shows all three with their words.
- [ ] A depends_on row shows the target id, title and the link reason.
- [ ] A stale card shows a red tint, keeps its status edge, and lists its trace under "Why this card is stale".
- [ ] A postponed card shows stripes and a postponed tag on its page and its tile.
- [ ] A tile is one link; a reference in its why line is not a nested link.
- [ ] A ghost sub-card appears after the real ones with a blue look.

## What to build

`werk say <id> "..."` appends one note to the thread of a card id. `werk inbox [--since <seq>] [-n 20]` prints the notes typed in the map in log order, starting after a log position and capped by count. A thread belongs to the card id, not to one proposal of it: a discard of a ghost leaves its thread in place, and when the same id is proposed again the thread shows a "proposed again" marker at that point, so notes above it were about the earlier words. A note on an excerpt is allowed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `werk say` adds a note that the thread of the card shows.
- [ ] `werk inbox --since` starts after the given log position, and `-n` caps the count.
- [ ] Discard and re-add of a ghost keeps its earlier notes and shows one marker between old and new notes.

## What to build

The map draws a ghost grey and dashed, under its proposed parent in the refines tree, distinct from a real card. A chain of ghosts nests the same way. The card view of a ghost has a ✓ button. Pressing it calls a server route that appends an accept event with the person as the actor, through the log module, and the map redraws from the log. The accept takes the ghost's proposed ancestors and its own options and sources with it.

Accept is one card at a time. There is no batch accept.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A ghost shows dashed and grey under its proposed parent, and a real card does not.
- [ ] Pressing ✓ on a ghost appends one accept event, and the card redraws as real.
- [ ] Pressing ✓ on a ghost child under a ghost parent makes both real.
- [ ] No control accepts more than one card that is not in the accepted card's proposed branch.

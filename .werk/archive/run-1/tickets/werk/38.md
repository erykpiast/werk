## What to build

`werk remove <id>` takes a card and its refines subtree out of the map. The fold drops them from every view and every derivation, and the log keeps every event. `--reparent` first lifts the direct children to the grandparent. `werk restore <id>` puts the card and its subtree back as they were. `werk removed` lists what is out.

`werk find <subtree-root>` lists the open cards in that subtree. It leaves out paused and stale cards, removed cards, and ghosts, so an agent can choose what to work on next.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a remove, the card and its refines subtree are gone from the fold and from `werk find`, and the log still holds every earlier event.
- [ ] A restore brings back the card, its subtree, and its links with the same status as before the remove.
- [ ] A remove with the reparent option leaves the children under the grandparent.
- [ ] `werk find` on a subtree with one open, one paused, one stale, and one decided card lists only the open card.
- [ ] `werk removed` lists every card that is out of the map.

## What to build

A building ticket with a live agent session pulses on the board. It stops the moment the session ends: a pass that exits, a failed retry that waits for the next attempt, or a stop by hand. The drawer of a building ticket shows a live preview of the agent's log above the slice, in a fixed-height scrolling viewport. When the ticket leaves building, the preview is gone.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A ticket with a running fake builder pulses, and stops pulsing when the pass exits.
- [ ] The drawer of that ticket shows the fake agent's events as they arrive.
- [ ] After the ticket moves to merging, its drawer shows no log preview.

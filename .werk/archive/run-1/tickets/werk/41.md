## What to build

The map view draws the fold as a refines tree, with the open card in the location hash. A click on a card opens its card view: title, why, body, status, rollup, and links. The page gets the graph from the read API and refetches it on every "changed" event, so a change from the command line shows with no manual refresh.

A ghost is drawn grey and dashed under its proposed parent, distinct from a real card. A ghost shows a ✓ button. A press appends the accept event through the server, with the person as the actor. A real card shows a remove button. Its confirmation names how many cards go, and a confirm appends the remove event. No view edits a drawn card in place.

A stale card shows a red mark, and a paused card a grey mark over its status colour. The card view shows the trace of each mark with its reopen reason.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A card added from the command line appears in the open page without a reload.
- [ ] A ghost shows grey and dashed under its parent, with a ✓. A press commits it, and the log gains one accept event with the person as the actor.
- [ ] The remove confirmation names the count of cards in the subtree. A confirm takes them out of the tree.
- [ ] A stale card shows red and a paused card shows grey with its status colour kept. The card view of each shows the reopen reason and the trace.
- [ ] The page has no control that edits a title, a why, or a body.
- [ ] Each browser test saves a screenshot.

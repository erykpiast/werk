## What to build

The remove verb takes a card and its refines subtree out of the map by appending one event. Nothing leaves the log. An option lifts the direct children to the grandparent first, for a bad parent over good children. The restore verb puts a removed card and its subtree back, and a removed-list verb shows what is out.

The find verb lists the open cards in a subtree. It leaves out paused and stale cards, so an agent can choose what to work on next.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a remove, the show verb reports the card and its children as removed, and the log still holds every earlier event.
- [ ] After a restore, the card and its subtree are back with their links.
- [ ] A remove with the lift option keeps the children in the map under the grandparent.
- [ ] The find verb on a subtree prints its open cards and omits a paused card and a stale card in it.

## What to build

The conversation manager reports running, waiting, and dead for each conversation, and keeps one permission ask at a time per conversation. A tab whose main turn ended but whose background agent still runs reports running.

A running tab shows a pulsing dot on its chip and on its home card in the tree. A tab with a pending ask blinks yellow. The '…' button blinks too when that tab is hidden behind it. The ask is answered only in that tab's own ask bar, never inline in another tab. A dead child shows red, and red wins over yellow on every surface. A child that dies in a background tab also shows a line in the focused pane that names the tab.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] While the fake runs a turn, its chip and its home card in the tree show a running dot. The dot stops when the turn and every background agent end.
- [ ] A fake that asks a permission question in a background tab makes that chip blink yellow, and the focused tab shows no ask bar.
- [ ] With that tab parked, the '…' button blinks.
- [ ] A switch to the asking tab shows its ask bar. An answer there reaches the fake.
- [ ] A fake that dies in a background tab turns its chip red and adds a line to the focused pane with the tab's home card id.
- [ ] A tab with both a pending ask and a dead child shows red.

## What to build

An agent reopens a decided card with a reason: postponed or rejected. The fold derives two kinds of mark from the log, in their own module.

A rejected reopen marks the next hop only as stale: the direct dependents and the direct children. A postponed reopen marks every downstream card, at any distance, as paused. A paused card keeps its status. When a postponement and an invalidation reach one card, stale wins. Each mark carries its trace: which change, along which link, and the reason.

The stale verb lists every marked card in the order to fix them, with the reopen reason of each mark. The rederive verb ends the review of one stale card with a confirm or an amend and clears its mark. A mark clears for paused cards when the postponed card is decided again.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A rejected reopen marks the direct dependent as stale, and a card two hops away carries no mark.
- [ ] A postponed reopen marks a card three hops away as paused, and its status stays as it was.
- [ ] A card reached by both a postponed and a rejected reopen shows as stale.
- [ ] The stale verb prints the reason of each mark and the card that caused it.
- [ ] The rederive verb clears the mark on one card and leaves the others.
- [ ] The staleness module is pure and on the pure-file list.

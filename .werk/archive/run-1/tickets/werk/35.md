## What to build

`werk link <from> <to> --type <t>` and `werk unlink` add or remove one typed link: refines, depends_on, supported_by, or alternative_to. A depends_on link carries a one-line why. `werk status` and the who setter work on a real card and on a ghost.

A link that names a ghost is held. The fold keeps held edges in their own set, and a held edge goes live by itself the moment both ends are real, so an accept needs no second event for its edges. A discard drops the held edges of every card it drops.

The fold derives a rollup on each real card (the status counts of its refines subtree) and the unchallenged count. A ghost and its held edges are outside every derivation, so a proposal does not change a number before the accept.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A link, a status change, and a who change on a ghost each succeed, and `werk show` prints them as held.
- [ ] After an accept of the ghost, its held edges are live, with no event other than the accept.
- [ ] A held edge between two ghosts stays held until both are accepted.
- [ ] A discard of a ghost removes its held edges from the fold.
- [ ] A ghost child does not change its real parent's rollup or the unchallenged count until it is accepted.
- [ ] The rollup derivation has unit tests and is on the pure-file list of the coverage gate.

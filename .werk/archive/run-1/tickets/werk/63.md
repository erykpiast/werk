## What to build

An agent proposes a card with its basis and its options as one unit. The link verb works on a ghost: an edge that names a ghost is held, not live. The fold keeps held edges in a set of their own, and an edge goes live by itself when both ends are real, so an accept needs no second event for its edges. The status verb and the who field also work on a ghost.

Accepting a card also accepts its ghost refines ancestors, up to the first real card, so a click cannot orphan a card. It also accepts the card's own ghost options and sources. It never accepts a depends_on target. The discard verb drops a ghost and every ghost descendant under it.

A ghost is outside every derivation: the rollup of a card, the staleness marks, and the unchallenged count do not change until the ghost is accepted.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A link between a ghost and a real card shows as held in the show verb output and is not a live edge.
- [ ] After the ghost is accepted, the same edge is live, and the log holds no extra link event.
- [ ] Accepting a ghost child under a ghost parent makes both real, and leaves a ghost depends_on target a ghost.
- [ ] Discarding a ghost parent also discards its ghost children.
- [ ] A ghost changes no rollup count and no unchallenged count of any real card.
- [ ] The status verb and the who field change a ghost without a refusal.

## What to build

The quote verb takes a session and a piece of text. The transcript reader finds the text verbatim in that conversation's transcript. On a single exact match, the verb proposes an excerpt node with the message index, the span, the speaker and the session, supported_by the card that cites it. The excerpt enters as a ghost, the same as every other kind.

Text that does not match exactly, or that matches more than one message, fails with an error and writes nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A quote that occurs once in a fixture transcript gives an excerpt with the right message index, span, speaker and session.
- [ ] The excerpt is a ghost linked supported_by from the citing card.
- [ ] A quote with one changed character exits non-zero and appends no event.
- [ ] A quote that occurs in two messages exits non-zero and names both messages.

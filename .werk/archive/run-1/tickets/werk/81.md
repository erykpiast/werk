## What to build

Ticket state is its own append-only log beside the decision log. The board fold, a pure module, folds it into five columns: blocked, ready, building, merging and done. The board view sits beside the map and draws one card per ticket.

A ticket card shows only its title, a retry count after more than one retry, and a count of its blocked_by tickets that are not done. A click on a ticket opens a drawer under the board: the slice on the left, and the metadata on the right (cited cards, blockers, retries, checks). A click on a cited card id in the drawer opens that card on the map.

A fixture board log in the test project stands in for the plan until the plan exists.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The fixture board shows each ticket in the column its last event names.
- [ ] A ticket with two retries shows a retry count, and one with one retry shows none.
- [ ] A ticket with two blockers, one of them done, shows a count of 1.
- [ ] A click on a ticket opens the drawer with the slice on the left and the metadata on the right.
- [ ] A click on a cited id in the drawer opens that card on the map.
- [ ] The board fold is on the pure-file list.

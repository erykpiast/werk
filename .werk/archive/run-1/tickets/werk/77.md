## What to build

At most five chips are visible. A sixth conversation parks the least recently touched idle tab. A tab with a running turn is never parked.

The strip ends with a '…' menu. It lists every conversation, marks the visible ones, the parked ones and the active one, and its button shows the total count. A click on a visible one switches to it. A click on a parked one un-parks it and parks the least recent idle tab; the un-park resumes the session from disk.

A tab's close control parks it at once and does not end the session. An idle check, at least once a minute, kills the process of a parked session that has been idle 30 minutes. Reopening it resumes from disk. The idle limit is a parameter.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Opening a sixth conversation leaves five chips and parks the least recently touched idle tab.
- [ ] A tab with a running fake-agent turn is never the one parked.
- [ ] The '…' menu lists all conversations with visible, parked and active marks, and its button shows the total.
- [ ] Un-parking a conversation resumes its session id with the resume option.
- [ ] The close control parks the tab, and the session stays in the menu.
- [ ] With the idle limit set to milliseconds, a parked session's child is killed, and reopening it starts a resumed child.

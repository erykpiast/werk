## What to build

`werk reopen <id>` takes a reason: postponed or rejected. The staleness derivation in the fold reads the order of the events. A rejected reopen marks the next hop only as stale: the direct depends_on dependents and the direct children. A postponed reopen marks every card downstream, at any distance, as paused, and keeps its status. If both reach one card, stale wins. A link made after the change carries no mark from it. `werk rederive <id>` clears the mark on one card with a confirm or an amend.

Every mark has a trace: which change, along which link, and why. `werk stale` lists every marked card in the order to fix them, with the reopen reason of each mark. `werk show` prints the trace of each mark on a card.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A rejected reopen of A marks B (B depends on A) stale and leaves C (C depends on B) unmarked.
- [ ] A postponed reopen of A marks B and C paused, and each keeps its status.
- [ ] A card that both a postponed and a rejected reopen reach shows stale.
- [ ] A depends_on link added after the reopen carries no mark.
- [ ] `werk stale` lists each marked card with the reopen reason and the trace, and `werk rederive` removes the card from that list.
- [ ] A reopen with no reason, or an unknown reason, exits non-zero.
- [ ] The staleness derivation has unit tests and is on the pure-file list of the coverage gate.

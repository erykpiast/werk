## What to build

The conversation owns its card and the map follows the focused tab. Focusing a tab moves the map to that tab's card. Navigating the map inside a tab updates only that tab's card and position, and switching away and back restores it. An unsent draft belongs to its tab and survives a switch.

Cmd+1 to Cmd+5 select a tab by position. Cmd+[ and Cmd+] step to the previous and next tab.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Focusing tab B moves the map to B's card, and the next turn in B starts with B's card.
- [ ] Navigating in tab A, switching to B and back restores A's card.
- [ ] A draft typed in A is gone in B and back in A.
- [ ] Cmd+2 focuses the second chip, and Cmd+] focuses the next one.

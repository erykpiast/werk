## What to build

The conversation manager can hold many conversations at the same time, one child each. A new conversation forks the parent session with the fork-session option, so it shares the parent's cached prefix. A second, explicit operation starts an empty conversation.

The conversation store records the session id and the home card of each conversation. The home card is the card open at the start. It names the conversation and seeds its first turn. It is not a fence: the conversation may navigate anywhere, and every turn still names the open card.

`werk convos` lists the sibling conversations with their home cards and session ids. `werk recall <session>` reads what a sibling said from its session log on disk. Nothing pushes a diff into a conversation. An agent reads a card from the map before it writes it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fork starts the fake with the resume and fork options on the parent's session id, and the store records the new session id with its home card.
- [ ] An empty start starts the fake with neither option.
- [ ] The first turn of a new conversation names its home card. A later turn after navigation names the open card.
- [ ] `werk convos` lists every conversation with its home card. `werk recall` prints the messages of a sibling's session log.
- [ ] Two conversations run turns at the same time, and each reply goes to its own conversation.

## What to build

A building ticket shows the archive action, the same as a ready or a blocked ticket. A merging or a done ticket does not show it. The confirmation says the ticket can be restored.

Archiving a building ticket ends its build: the builder session stops, the pulse ends, and the ticket's branch stays. The ticket restore verb brings the ticket back.

No other move returns a building ticket to ready. A drag from building into ready is refused, and nothing stops a build in place.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A building ticket shows the archive action, and a merging ticket and a done ticket do not.
- [ ] Archiving a building ticket with a running fake builder asks for confirmation, and the confirmation says the ticket can be restored.
- [ ] After the archive, the fake builder process is gone, the ticket stops pulsing, and its branch still exists.
- [ ] The ticket restore verb brings the archived ticket back onto the board.
- [ ] A drag of a building ticket into ready is refused, and the build goes on.

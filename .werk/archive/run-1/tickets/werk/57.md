## What to build

The board gives four direct actions, each one an event through the server. A person reorders tickets within ready by drag. A ready or blocked ticket shows an archive action. Its confirmation says the ticket is never deleted for good and can be restored. A ticket in building, merging, or done shows no archive action. Inside an active run, a ready ticket shows a trigger icon. A press, or a drag into building, starts its build at once, and it then goes through the same merge queue. A drag of a building ticket back into ready stops its build.

No other move is a person's: a drag to any other column is refused. The board has no control that edits a ticket's text.

A building ticket pulses while its agent session runs and stops the moment the session ends. Its drawer shows a live preview of the session log above the slice, in a fixed-height scrolling box. The preview goes away when the ticket leaves building.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A drag within ready changes the order, and the scheduler takes the new order.
- [ ] An archive shows a confirmation that says the ticket can be restored. A confirm moves it off the board. Building, merging, and done tickets show no archive action.
- [ ] In an active run, the trigger icon and a drag into building each start that ticket's build. Outside an active run, both are refused.
- [ ] A drag of a building ticket into ready stops its fake builder and puts the ticket in ready.
- [ ] A drag into merging or done is refused, and the ticket stays.
- [ ] A building ticket pulses while the fake session runs and stops when it exits. The drawer shows the session's lines as they come, and the preview is gone after the ticket leaves building.
- [ ] The drawer has no control that edits the ticket.

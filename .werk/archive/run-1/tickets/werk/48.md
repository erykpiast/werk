## What to build

Ticket state is its own append-only board log beside the decision log. It records each ticket's column, its order in ready, its retry count, its archive with a reason, its restore, and a held-out badge that links a done ticket to a follow-up ticket. The board fold is a pure function from that log and the ticket files to the five columns: blocked, ready, building, merging, and done.

The board view shows one card per ticket. A card shows only its title, a retry count after more than one retry, and a count of its blocked_by tickets that are not done. A done ticket with a red held-out step after its merge shows a badge that links to the follow-up ticket.

A click on a ticket opens a drawer under the board. The slice, rendered as Markdown, is on the left. The metadata is on the right: cited cards, blocking tickets, stories, retries, and checks. A click on a cited card id opens that card on the map. The board refetches on every "changed" event.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A board log written by a test shows each ticket in its column, in the order the log gives.
- [ ] A ticket card shows its title. It shows a retry count only after two retries, and a count of unmet blocking tickets only when one is not done.
- [ ] A done ticket with a held-out badge shows the badge on the card and in the drawer, and the badge links to the follow-up ticket.
- [ ] A click on a ticket opens the drawer with the slice on the left and the metadata on the right.
- [ ] A click on a cited id in the drawer opens that card on the map.
- [ ] A board log event appended while the page is open moves the ticket without a reload.
- [ ] The board fold has unit tests and is on the pure-file list of the coverage gate.

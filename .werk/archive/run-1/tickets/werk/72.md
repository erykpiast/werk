## What to build

The map draws a stale mark in red and a paused mark in grey on top of the card's status colour, which stays. The card view of a marked card shows its trace under the heading "Why this card is stale": the change, the link, and the reopen reason.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a rejected reopen from the command line, the direct dependent shows a red mark in the map.
- [ ] After a postponed reopen, a card two hops away shows a grey mark and keeps its status colour.
- [ ] The card view of a marked card names the reopened card and the reason.

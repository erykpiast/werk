## What to build

Each conversation reports running, waiting or dead. A running tab has a running dot on its chip and on its home card in the map tree. A tab whose main turn ended but which still has a background agent going reads as running. A permission ask stays on its own tab, in that tab's ask bar, one ask at a time; another tab never shows it. A tab with a pending ask blinks yellow, and the '…' button blinks too when that tab is parked behind it. A dead child shows red, and red wins over yellow on every surface. When a background tab's child dies, a line that names the tab appears in the pane the person is looking at.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] During a fake-agent turn, the chip and the home card in the tree show the running dot, and both stop when the turn and its background agents end.
- [ ] A tab whose main turn ended while a background agent runs still shows running.
- [ ] A permission ask from a background tab appears only in that tab's ask bar, and its chip blinks yellow.
- [ ] With the asking tab parked, the '…' button blinks.
- [ ] A tab with a dead child and a pending ask shows red, not yellow.
- [ ] When a background tab's child exits, the focused pane shows a line with that tab's name.

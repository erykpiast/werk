## What to build

The strip shows at most five chips. A sixth conversation parks the least recently touched idle tab. A tab with a running turn is never parked.

A '…' menu at the end of the strip lists every conversation: visible ones and parked ones marked apart, the active one marked too. Its button shows the total count. A click on a visible one switches to it. A click on a parked one un-parks it and parks the least recent idle tab. A tab's close control parks it at once. Nothing ends a session for good.

A parked conversation whose child has been idle for 30 minutes has its child killed. A reopen resumes the session from disk with the resume option. The idle limit is a parameter.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With six conversations, five chips show, and the parked one is the least recently touched idle tab.
- [ ] A conversation with a running turn is never parked, even when it is the least recent.
- [ ] The '…' button shows the total count, and its menu lists every conversation with visible, parked, and active marks.
- [ ] A close parks the tab and its session id stays in the store.
- [ ] With the idle limit set to milliseconds, a parked child is killed after the limit. A reopen starts the fake with the resume option on the same session id.

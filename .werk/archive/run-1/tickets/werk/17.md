## What to build

werk v0 is one app with three views: the map, the board and the chat pane beside them, and a person moves between the map and the board. The three parts of v0 — the log, the fold and the web view — carry the last two map verbs. The find operation lists the open cards in a subtree and leaves out paused and stale cards, so an agent can choose what to work on next. The remove operation takes a card and its refines subtree out of the map with one event. A restore puts it back. The map has a remove control on the card view; its confirmation names how many cards go, and a click appends the remove event. Removed cards stay in the log and can be listed. The product diagram, the 3D graph, the Flow view, the tracker export, the validate stage and pipeline self-measurement are not in v0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Find on a subtree prints the open cards in it, and a paused or stale card in that subtree is not in the output.
- [ ] Remove on a card with two refines children takes all three out of the map, and the log still holds their events.
- [ ] Restore of that card brings back all three with their links.
- [ ] The remove control in the browser names the number of cards in its confirmation, and confirming it appends one event.
- [ ] The list of removed cards shows the removed card.
- [ ] The app shows the map, the board and the chat pane, and a person moves between the map and the board.
- [ ] The app has no product diagram, 3D graph, Flow view or tracker export.

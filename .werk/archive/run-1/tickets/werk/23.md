## What to build

When the builder starts on a ticket, a step author agent writes held-out steps from the brief: each one a whole user path through the product, as Given/When/Then text. A binder agent turns the steps into tests and never makes a test pass. The steps and tests live outside the builder's worktree, prompt and checks. The held-out set runs on the run's tree. When a step fails, the builder gets the text of the failed step. The G2 gate refuses while any held-out step is red on the run's tree.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A step author starts when the builder starts, and its steps are Given/When/Then text.
- [ ] No builder prompt and no builder worktree contains a held-out step or test.
- [ ] A red held-out step gives the builder the step's text and nothing more.
- [ ] G2 refuses while one held-out step is red, and opens when every step is green.
- [ ] No builder check runs a held-out step or test.

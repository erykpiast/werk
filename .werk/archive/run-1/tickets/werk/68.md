## What to build

The say verb appends a note to a card's thread in the log. The inbox verb prints the notes, grouped by card, so that an agent can read a whole thread and answer on the card and not only in the terminal. A note works on a real card and on a ghost.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The say verb appends one note event that names the card, and the show verb prints the note.
- [ ] The inbox verb prints every note of a card in order.
- [ ] The say verb on an unknown card id exits non-zero and appends nothing.

## What to build

The conversation manager reports running, waiting and dead per conversation. A tab with a background agent still going reads as running after its main turn ends.

Each conversation keeps one permission ask at a time, answered only in that tab's own ask bar. Another tab never shows it.

A running tab shows a running dot on its chip and on its home card in the map tree. A tab with a pending ask blinks yellow, and the '…' button blinks when that tab is hidden behind it. A dead child shows red; red wins over yellow on every surface. A dead child in a background tab also pushes a line, with the tab's name, into the pane the person is looking at.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fake-agent ask in a background tab makes that chip blink yellow and adds nothing to the focused pane.
- [ ] The ask bar of that tab shows the ask, and the answer reaches only that child.
- [ ] A parked tab with an ask makes the '…' button blink.
- [ ] A running tab shows a dot on its chip and on its home card in the tree; a tab with a scripted background agent still shows it after the main turn ends.
- [ ] Killing a background child shows a red dot, even with an ask pending, and a line in the focused pane that names the tab.

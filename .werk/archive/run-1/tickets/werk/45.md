## What to build

The tab strip is the last row of the chat pane, with tabs flush to the pane edges. Each chip shows the home card id and a state dot. The active chip joins the area above it.

A plain click on a card moves the map and keeps the current conversation. Shift+click on a card forks a conversation rooted there and focuses it. Shift+click on a card that already has a tab switches to that tab. A '+' at the end of the strip forks at the open card. Cmd+1 to Cmd+5 select a tab by position, and Cmd+[ and Cmd+] step.

The conversation owns its card: focusing a tab moves the map to that tab's card. Each tab keeps its own map position, so switching away and back restores it. An unsent draft belongs to its tab.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A plain click on a card moves the map and the active tab stays the same.
- [ ] Shift+click on a card adds a chip with that card id, focuses it, and the map shows that card.
- [ ] Shift+click on the same card again focuses the existing tab and adds no chip.
- [ ] '+' adds a chip for the open card and the map does not move.
- [ ] Cmd+2 focuses the second chip, and Cmd+] focuses the next one.
- [ ] Tab A at card X, tab B at card Y: a switch to A shows X, and a switch to B shows Y.
- [ ] Text typed in tab A and not sent is back in the composer after a switch to B and back to A.
- [ ] The strip is the last row of the pane and its first chip starts at the pane's left edge.

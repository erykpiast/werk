## What to build

The strip shows at most five chips. A sixth conversation parks the least recently touched idle tab; a tab with a running turn is never parked. A '…' menu at the end of the strip lists every conversation, marks the visible, parked and active ones, and shows the total count. A click on a visible entry switches to it. A click on a parked entry un-parks it by resuming its session from disk, and parks whatever is now least recent. A tab's close control parks it at once and does not end the session. A parked session's process is killed after 30 minutes idle, and reopening it resumes the session. The idle time is a parameter, 30 minutes by default.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With five idle tabs, a sixth fork parks the least recently touched one.
- [ ] With five tabs where the oldest has a running turn, a sixth fork parks a different, idle tab.
- [ ] The '…' button shows the total number of conversations, and the menu marks visible, parked and active entries.
- [ ] Un-parking a conversation resumes its session id, and its history is in the pane.
- [ ] The close control moves a tab into the parked list, and its session stays resumable.
- [ ] With the idle limit set to milliseconds, a parked child process is killed, and reopening the tab starts a resumed child.

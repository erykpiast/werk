## What to build

`werk show [<id>]` prints what a person sees on the card page. The rollup over the card's refines subtree, the card included (cards, open, proto, decided, unchallenged, stale), prints as one line for the card and again on each incoming refines link. The rollup has one definition, in the fold, which the server will later reuse. Depends on and depend on this card list each link with its reason. Each mark prints its trace. After the links come up to three sections: Options (title and why-not), Say (speaker and verbatim words of each excerpt the card is supported_by), Ev (title and finding of each source). A ghost item carries the word proposed. An empty section is not printed. With no id, show prints the root card.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rollup line counts the card and its subtree, and adds up across open, proto and decided.
- [ ] Each incoming refines link prints the child's rollup.
- [ ] A card with an option, an excerpt and a ghost source prints all three sections with their words, the source marked proposed.
- [ ] A card with no options prints no Options section.

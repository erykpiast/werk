## What to build

`werk remove <id> --actor you` takes a card out of the map with its refines subtree and the options and excerpts of what it removes; a source stays while any remaining card cites it. With `--reparent`, the direct children move up to the grandparent and only the card goes. The removal is one event. A removed card is in no view, makes nothing stale, counts in no rollup and cites nothing.

`werk restore <id> --actor you` is the exact inverse of the removal it names: the card, its subtree, its options and sources come back, and children moved up by a reparent go back under it. A node that went out with another card comes back only through that card. A branch removed earlier stays out when a card above it is removed and restored. `werk removed` lists what is out. Without `--actor you`, remove and restore refuse.

While a card is out its links wait: a link is out while either end is out and comes back when both are in, whatever the order of removals and restores. A removed card's id stays taken: an add with that id refuses and names the restore.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Removing a card with two children removes three cards in one event; restoring brings all three back with their links.
- [ ] Remove with reparent moves the children to the grandparent, and a restore puts them back under the card.
- [ ] Removing login, then session, then restoring login keeps the link between them out until session is restored.
- [ ] Adding a card with a removed card's id is refused and names the restore.
- [ ] A source cited by a remaining card stays after the removal.
- [ ] Remove and restore without `--actor you` refuse and append nothing.

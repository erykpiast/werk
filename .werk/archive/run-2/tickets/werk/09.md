## What to build

`werk search` with no term prints the index of decided cards, one line per card with id and title. With terms, a card matches when every term occurs, case-insensitive, in its title, why, body or one of its options' why-not; one line per match with id, status and title, the most hits first. `--status` filters by status (default decided with no term, every status with a term) and `--under <id>` keeps one refines subtree. A ghost or removed card never matches.

`werk find [<id>]` lists real cards with status open or proto in the subtree of a card, breadth first, the card itself included, or in every tree of the map with no id. It leaves out options, sources, excerpts, decided cards, and every card marked paused, stale or postponed. A card reopened as rejected is listed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `werk search` with no term lists every decided card and no ghost.
- [ ] A search with two terms returns only cards that hold both, ordered by hits.
- [ ] A term found only in an option's why-not matches its card.
- [ ] `werk find` lists open and proto cards under a card and leaves out paused, stale and postponed ones.
- [ ] A card reopened as rejected appears in find.

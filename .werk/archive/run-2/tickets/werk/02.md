## What to build

A person or an agent runs `werk help` and sees every verb with its flags. The list comes from the verbs that registered themselves: each verb is its own module that registers a name, its flags, its help text and its handler. Adding a verb adds one module and one entry in the shared list of verb modules, and edits no other verb.

The start-up of the command line loads the verbs, starts the chosen verb and does not wait for it as part of its own evaluation; when the verb ends, a callback exits with the verb's exit code. An unknown verb prints the help and exits 2.

Every argument that names a card accepts the id with or without one leading `#`: a shared helper strips exactly one leading hash from positional ids and from flags whose value is a card id. Text arguments such as a title, why, body or note keep their hash signs.

This ticket ships with one trivial verb (for example `version`) to show the registry working end to end.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `werk help` lists every registered verb with its flags, and no hard-coded list exists apart from the entries of verb modules.
- [ ] A test registers a second verb from its own module and sees it in the help output without touching any other verb.
- [ ] An unknown verb prints the help and exits 2.
- [ ] The start-up returns before the verb finishes, and the process exits with the verb's exit code.
- [ ] The card id helper turns `#auth` and `auth` into `auth`, and leaves `##auth` as `#auth`.
- [ ] A text flag such as a title starting with `#` is passed through unchanged.

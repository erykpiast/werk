## What to build

A plan over a subtree that already has a spec and tickets extends them. It updates the one spec, rewrites the ready and blocked tickets in place under their ids, adds new tickets after the highest number, and leaves building, merging and done tickets alone. An open ticket that delivers nothing in the new spec is archived with the reason that the current spec no longer needs it; a restore brings it back.

When a plan puts a ticket on the board, the board records the version of each card it cites. werk watches the decision log; on each change it finds the open tickets that cite the changed card. A new title, why or body, a status change, a removal, a new stale mark, or an added or removed depends_on counts; a note does not. Each such ready or blocked ticket moves to blocked with a reason naming the card and the kind of change; building, merging and done tickets are untouched. A plan that keeps or changes the ticket against the new versions clears the mark. A card added under a planned subtree reruns the cards-cited check and reports a failure without blocking any ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A second plan of the same subtree leaves one spec and no duplicate tickets, and new tickets continue the numbering.
- [ ] An open ticket the new spec does not need is archived with the reason and can be restored.
- [ ] Retitling a card cited by a ready ticket moves that ticket to blocked with the card named.
- [ ] A note on that card blocks nothing; a building ticket is never blocked.
- [ ] A re-plan clears the block.

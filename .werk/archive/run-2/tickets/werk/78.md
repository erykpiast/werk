## What to build

When `werk build` starts, before any code is written, it appends a card obligation for that build to the decision log. The obligation is data, not a card: it is in no view of the map and in no rollup. Each pass's prompt names the obligation and tells the builder the two ways to close it: propose one card for each decision that no cited card and no searched card gives, or record that the pass made no new decision and name the decided cards its choices came from.

A pass that proposes a card closes the obligation with that card. `werk followed <obligation> --cards a,b` closes it with the record "no new decision" and the named cards, printing the obligation id and the cards; it is a fact in the log and needs no accept. A call without cards, a card that is a ghost, removed, unknown or not decided, an unknown obligation, or one already closed is refused with exit 1, names what is wrong, and writes nothing. An obligation that a build leaves open blocks nothing.

The fold exposes, per ticket, the cards its builds proposed (with proposal or accepted) and the cards a record followed, so the board's ticket drawer and the grader can read them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Starting a build appends one obligation before the first pass, and the pass prompt names it.
- [ ] `werk followed` on an open obligation with a decided card closes it and exits 0.
- [ ] A second `werk followed` on the same obligation exits 1 and names what closed it.
- [ ] A ghost or undecided card in the list is refused and nothing is written.
- [ ] A fake builder that proposes a card closes the obligation with that card.
- [ ] The fold lists, per ticket, the proposed cards with their state and the followed cards.

## What to build

Every change to a project's map is one event appended to its decision log, and the map is a pure fold of that log, run on every read with its fold time recorded. The fold holds the latest title, why, body and links of each node.

`werk add` proposes a card, an option or a source as a ghost, with its parent in the same single event; it prints "proposed" and refuses `--status decided`. A ghost's words are frozen: a second add with the same id refuses. `werk link` and `werk unlink` add or remove one typed link (refines, depends_on, supported_by, alternative_to); a link on a ghost is held until both ends are real. `werk accept <id> --actor you` makes a ghost real, with its ghost refines parents and the ghost options, sources and excerpts it is supported_by; without `--actor you` it refuses. `werk discard` drops a ghost, its ghost descendants and its own excerpts, and drops a ghost source only when no remaining card cites it. A minimal `werk show` prints one node with its status, who, parent and links.

The map rules live in one pure module: one parent per card, no cycle, an option has only alternative_to, a depends_on needs a reason, a depends_on to an ancestor or descendant is refused, ghost words are frozen. A write checks all its events against the folded map and appends all or none, naming the rule it breaks. The fold applies the same check to each committed line; a bad line is set aside as a problem with its log position, and the lines after it still fold. Ghosts sit outside every derivation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Adding a card under a parent appends exactly one event, and the card shows under its parent as a ghost.
- [ ] A refused write appends nothing and prints the rule it breaks.
- [ ] A hand-inserted bad line in the log is listed as a problem with its position, and later events still fold.
- [ ] A depends_on link without a reason, or to an ancestor or descendant, is refused.
- [ ] Accepting a ghost child also accepts its ghost parents and its ghost sources and options; its held links go live.
- [ ] Accept without `--actor you` exits non-zero and appends nothing.
- [ ] Discarding a ghost keeps a ghost source that another card still cites and drops one that only it cited.
- [ ] The fold module and the rules module are on the pure list and pass the coverage gate.

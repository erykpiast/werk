## What to build

The map opens on the page of the root card, and every card has a page of the same shape. The hero box has a tag row (the id that copies itself, kind, status, each mark as a word, the who label), a heading that shrinks with depth, the why and body as Markdown with live links, and the rollup: a bar of decided, open and proto widths with a labelled count line, plus separate chips for stale and unchallenged.

Below the hero come the sections: why this card is stale (one trace per mark), options, evidence and excerpts with their words, depends on and depend on this card (id, title, link reason), then the sub-cards as tiles. A tile is one link: title, why line, a meta row with id, counts, who and ring, and a rollup bar; real tiles first, ghosts last.

Every card box has a status-colored left edge; stale tints the background red; a postponed card has grey diagonal stripes, a grey postponed tag, and a hatched slice in its parents' bars.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The root page lists its children as tiles, with real cards before ghosts.
- [ ] A stale card's page shows its trace under "Why this card is stale".
- [ ] A card page shows each option with its why-not and each excerpt with its words.
- [ ] A postponed card shows the postponed tag and stripes, and its left edge keeps its status color.
- [ ] The count line says "this card and everything below it" with the total.

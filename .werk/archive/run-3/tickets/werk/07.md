## What to build

Every card, option, source and excerpt that an agent adds is a ghost: in the log, outside every rollup and mark, with its edges held. Its words are frozen: an edit of body, why or title refuses on a ghost.

Accept commits one ghost and refuses unless the call states that the person asked for it. It also commits the ghost refines ancestors up to the first real card, and every ghost source and excerpt that the accepted cards are supported_by. Discard drops a ghost with its ghost descendants and their ghost excerpts, and drops a ghost source only when no remaining card cites it.

The say verb appends a note to a card's thread, and the inbox verb prints the notes with an optional start position and count. A thread belongs to the id: a discard keeps it, and a new proposal under the same id shows a "proposed again" marker in the thread.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Accept without the person's statement exits non-zero and appends nothing.
- [ ] Accepting a ghost child under a ghost parent makes both real, and their held edges go live.
- [ ] Accepting a card makes its ghost source real; a discard keeps a ghost source that another ghost cites and drops one that only the discarded card cites.
- [ ] Editing the body of a ghost is refused; link and who on a ghost work.
- [ ] After discard and a new add with the same id, inbox and show list the old note, then the marker, then new notes.
- [ ] The proposal rules pass the coverage gate.

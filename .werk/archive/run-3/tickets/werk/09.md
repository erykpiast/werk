## What to build

The propose verb stages a change to the title, the why or the body of a card as a list of tagged changes: elaborated with an old and a new block, new, or removed. Every block of the current body must be the old of a change; otherwise the proposal is refused and the blocks at risk are printed. An old that quotes no real block is shown as removed plus new, never trusted. The pending change is the latest proposal not yet accepted or discarded, and the accept and discard verbs of proposals apply to it.

A decided card is frozen in its title, why, body, depends_on links and parent. An accepted change to a frozen field is an amend: one step that reopens and decides the card, so the map never shows it open. The amend runs the four facts on the new version. Title, why, body, depends_on and parent changes need all four facts after the change. Any other change is refused only when it removes a fact the card had, so a missing fact can always be added. The diff that the show verb prints marks added and removed words inside elaborated blocks.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A proposal that leaves one body block unnamed is refused and prints that block.
- [ ] Accepting an edit to a decided card leaves it decided, with the new body.
- [ ] Removing the last option of a decided you-card is refused; adding a second option and then removing the first works.
- [ ] A card decided with no kill criterion refuses a body edit and accepts a new kill criterion.
- [ ] The edit-diff module passes the coverage gate.

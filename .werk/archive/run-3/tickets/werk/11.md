## What to build

The remove verb takes a card out of the map with its refines subtree, its options and its excerpts, and with every source that only that branch cites. A reparent option instead removes only the card and moves its direct children to its parent. A removal by an agent is a proposal that the person accepts. A removed card is out of every view, rollup and mark, and cites nothing. Nothing leaves the log.

The restore verb undoes one removal exactly, the moved children too. A node that went out with another card comes back only through that card. A link waits while either end is out and is back when both ends are in. While a card is out its id stays taken: an add with that id refuses and names the restore. A removed list prints what is out.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Removing a parent hides its children and its options, and restoring it brings them back with their links.
- [ ] A reparent removal moves the children to the grandparent; the restore moves them back.
- [ ] With both ends of a depends_on link removed, restoring one end keeps the link out; restoring the other brings it back.
- [ ] Adding a card with the id of a removed card is refused and names the restore.
- [ ] A source cited by a card outside the removed branch stays.
- [ ] The removal rules pass the coverage gate.

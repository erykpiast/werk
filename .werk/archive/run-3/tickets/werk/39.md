## What to build

The merge loop starts a ticket when every blocker is merged and no failed set check holds it, and builds up to three such tickets at the same time, each in its own worktree branched off the main line's tip at its start. It starts a ticket beside the ones already building only when their touches names share none, compared trimmed and in lower case. A ticket that shares a name waits until the other merges and then builds on top of it. The merge queue still takes one ticket at a time.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Three independent tickets with distinct touches run three builder sessions at once.
- [ ] Two tickets that both touch "Board View" and "board view" never build at the same time.
- [ ] A fourth ready ticket waits until one of three running builds ends.
- [ ] A ticket that starts after a merge has that merge in its worktree.

## What to build

The reopen verb takes a reason, postponed, invalidated (the default) or rejected, and a text. The fold derives the marks from the order of events; nothing writes them. Invalidated, rejected and an amend mark the next hop only, the direct dependents and direct children, as stale. A postponement marks every card downstream, at any distance, as paused, over the links that existed at that moment; the postponed card carries the postponed mark itself. Stale wins where both reach. A link made after a change carries no mark from it. A card whose basis is open or stale waits and is not stale. A paused card is still a basis that holds. Deciding a postponed card again clears the paused marks it caused.

Each mark carries a trace: the origin, the reason or "amended", the date, the text, and the link with its reason; a paused trace also names the cards between, nearest first, and the last link type.

The rederive verb ends a review: a confirm clears the mark only when the basis is decided and not stale, and every outgoing link reason is confirmed; it can also decide the card through the same contract check. An amend at the end of a review marks the next hop. The stale verb lists marked cards, with an optional subtree and kind filter, in fix order, with the trace line of each. Show prints the trace of each mark.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reopening X with no reason makes its direct dependent Y stale and leaves Y's dependent Z clear.
- [ ] Postponing P pauses a card three hops down, and that card's trace names the two cards between.
- [ ] A card linked to a stale card after the change waits and carries no mark.
- [ ] A confirm on a card whose basis is still stale is refused with the fixing command.
- [ ] Amending Y at the end of its review marks Z stale with the trace "amended" and the review sentence.
- [ ] The stale verb prints a basis before the card that depends on it, and exits 0 with a message when nothing is marked.
- [ ] Mark derivation passes the coverage gate.

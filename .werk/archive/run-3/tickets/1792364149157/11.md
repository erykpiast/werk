## What to build

The conversation owns its card, and the map follows the focused tab. Focusing a tab moves the map to that tab's card. Navigating the map inside a tab updates only that tab's card, so switching away and back restores it. An unsent draft belongs to its tab and survives a switch. Each turn starts with the focused tab's card.

A plain click on a card moves the map and keeps the current conversation. Shift+click on a card forks a conversation rooted there and focuses it. Shift+click on a card that already has a tab switches to that tab. A '+' at the end of the tab strip forks at the open card.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A plain click moves the map and keeps the active tab. A running turn in that tab keeps running.
- [ ] Shift+click on a card with no tab creates one tab homed on that card, focuses it, and moves the map there.
- [ ] Shift+click on a card that has a tab focuses the existing tab and creates none.
- [ ] '+' forks at the open card and leaves the map where it is.
- [ ] Switching from tab A to tab B and back restores A's card and A's draft.
- [ ] A turn sent from a tab starts with that tab's card, not the previous tab's card.

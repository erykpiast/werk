## What to build

Every add from an agent — a card, an option, a source, or an excerpt — appends its own "proposed" event type, not a flag on an added card. Its output says "proposed". The fold keeps the ghost in the nodes with a proposed flag and outside every derivation: live edges, rollups, staleness marks, and the unchallenged count. The ghost's own edges wait in a separate held set and go live by themselves when both ends are real.

The map draws a ghost grey and dashed under its proposed parent, apart from the blue tint of a pending edit on a real card. A ✓ on the ghost accepts that one card. An accept also accepts the ghost refines ancestors up to the first real card, and the card's own ghost options and sources. A discard removes the card and its ghost descendants.

The words of a ghost are frozen: a change to the title, the body, or the why refuses, and so does a second add with the same id. A link, a status, and the who field still work on a ghost. An accept from the command line refuses unless it states that the person asked for it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An add of each kind (card, option, source, excerpt) appends a "proposed" event and prints "proposed".
- [ ] A ghost shows grey and dashed under its proposed parent in the browser, and the ✓ on it makes it real.
- [ ] A ghost does not change any rollup, staleness mark, or unchallenged count until it is accepted.
- [ ] Accept of a ghost child with a ghost parent makes both real. Discard of a ghost parent removes its ghost descendants.
- [ ] A retitle, an edit of the body or the why, and a second add on a ghost each refuse with a non-zero exit code.
- [ ] A link and a status on a ghost succeed. The held edge goes live at the accept, with no second event.
- [ ] An accept from the command line without the actor claim refuses. With the claim it succeeds.
- [ ] There is no batch accept.

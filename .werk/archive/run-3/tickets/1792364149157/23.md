## What to build

A board view sits beside the map. It folds the board state into one card per ticket. A ticket card shows only its title, a retry count after more than one retry, and a count of its unmet blocking tickets.

A click on a ticket opens a drawer under the board. The slice — what to build and the acceptance criteria — is in the main column on the left. The metadata and relations — cited cards, blocking tickets, retries, stories, checks — are in a sidebar on the right. A click on a cited card id opens that card on the map.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a plan, the board shows one card per ticket with only its title.
- [ ] A ticket with two unmet blocking tickets shows the count 2. A ticket with two retries shows a retry count.
- [ ] A click on a ticket opens the drawer with the slice on the left and the metadata on the right.
- [ ] A click on a cited card id in the drawer opens that card on the map.
- [ ] The board fold has unit tests and is on the pure-file list of the coverage gate.

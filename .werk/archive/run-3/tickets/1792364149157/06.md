## What to build

A reopen of a card from the command line takes a reason: postponed or rejected. The fold derives marks on the cards that depend on it. A rejected reopen marks the next hop only — the direct dependents and the direct children — as stale, in red. A postponed reopen marks every downstream card, at any distance, as paused, in grey, and keeps the status colour under the mark. When both reach one card, stale wins. Each mark keeps its trace: which change, along which link, and the reason.

A review of a stale card clears its mark and lets the next hop be marked in turn. Resuming postponed work clears the paused marks.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A reopen without a reason refuses.
- [ ] A rejected reopen marks only the next hop as stale. A card two hops down has no mark.
- [ ] A postponed reopen marks every downstream card as paused, at any distance.
- [ ] A card reached by both a postponement and an invalidation shows stale.
- [ ] In the browser, a stale card shows red and a paused card shows grey with its status colour kept.
- [ ] The staleness listing on the command line prints each mark with its reason and trace.
- [ ] The mark derivation has unit tests on the pure-file list.

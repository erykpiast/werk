## What to build

The board has five columns: blocked, ready, building, merging, and done. A ticket moves right as the loop moves it. The builder loop moves a ticket into building, the merge queue into merging, and a merge into done. A red check in merging sends the ticket back to building by itself. An agent moves tickets between blocked and ready.

The held-out set does not move a ticket. A red held-out step shows as a badge on the done ticket whose merge it followed, on the card and in its drawer metadata, linked to the follow-up ticket. The follow-up ticket appears in ready.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A run with the fake agents moves a ticket through ready, building, merging, and done, and the board shows each move live.
- [ ] A red check in merging puts the ticket back in building with no person action.
- [ ] A red held-out step shows a badge on the done ticket whose merge it followed, linked to a follow-up ticket in ready.
- [ ] Every move on the board comes from an event in the board state.

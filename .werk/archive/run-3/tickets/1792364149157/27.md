## What to build

werk v0 is one app with three views: the map, the board, and the chat pane beside them. An agent runs a find operation that lists the open cards in a subtree and leaves out paused and stale cards, to choose what to work on next. A remove takes a card and its refines subtree out of the map, and a restore brings the whole subtree back. The staleness view lists every marked card with the reopen reason of its mark.

The product diagram, the 3D graph, the Flow view, the tracker export, the validate stage, and pipeline self-measurement are not in v0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The app shows the map, the board, and the chat pane, and a person moves between the map and the board.
- [ ] Find on a subtree lists its open cards and leaves out paused and stale cards.
- [ ] Remove on a card takes it and its refines subtree out of the map, and restore puts all of them back.
- [ ] The staleness view shows each mark with its reason: postponed or rejected.
- [ ] The app has no product diagram, 3D graph, Flow view, or tracker export.

## What to build

The board is a view beside the map. It reads the ticket state log, not card status, and draws one card per ticket in the column of its state. A ticket card shows only its title, a retry count after more than one retry, and a count of its unmet blocking tickets. A click on a ticket opens a drawer under the board: the slice on the left, and the metadata on the right — cited cards, blocking tickets, retries, checks. A click on a cited card id opens that card on the map.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a plan on the fixture project, the board shows each ticket in its column with only its title.
- [ ] A ticket blocked by two tickets that are not done shows the count 2.
- [ ] A ticket with one retry shows no retry count, and a ticket with two retries shows one.
- [ ] A click on a ticket opens the drawer with the slice on the left and the metadata on the right.
- [ ] A click on a cited id in the drawer opens that card on the map.
- [ ] The board fold is on the pure-file list of the coverage gate.

## What to build

The conversation owns its card, and the map follows the focused tab. Focusing a tab moves the map to that tab's card. Navigating inside a tab updates only that tab's position, and switching away and back restores it. An unsent draft belongs to its tab. Every turn starts with the focused tab's card.

A plain click on a card moves the map and keeps the current conversation. Shift+click on a card forks a conversation rooted there and focuses it. Shift+click on a card that already has a tab switches to that tab. A '+' at the end of the strip forks at the open card.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A plain click while a turn runs moves the map and leaves the same tab focused, and the turn keeps running.
- [ ] Shift+click on a card opens a new focused tab with that card as home, and the map shows that card.
- [ ] A second Shift+click on the same card focuses the existing tab and makes no second fork.
- [ ] '+' forks at the open card, and the map does not move.
- [ ] After moving the map in tab A, switching to tab B and back restores tab A's position.
- [ ] A draft typed in tab A is still there after a switch to tab B and back, and tab B has its own draft.
- [ ] A turn sent from tab B starts with tab B's card, not the card tab A showed.

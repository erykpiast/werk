## What to build

The three parts of v0 — the log, the fold and the web view — carry the last two map verbs. The find operation lists the open cards in a subtree and leaves out paused and stale cards, so an agent can choose what to work on next. The remove operation takes a card and its refines subtree out of the map with one event. A restore puts it back. The map has a remove control on the card view; its confirmation names how many cards go, and a click appends the remove event. Removed cards stay in the log and can be listed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Find on a subtree prints the open cards in it, and a paused or stale card in that subtree is not in the output.
- [ ] Remove on a card with two refines children takes all three out of the map, and the log still holds their events.
- [ ] Restore of that card brings back all three with their links.
- [ ] The remove control in the browser names the number of cards in its confirmation, and confirming it appends one event.
- [ ] The list of removed cards shows the removed card.

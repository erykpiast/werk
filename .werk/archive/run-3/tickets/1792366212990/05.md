## What to build

A reopen takes a reason: postponed or rejected. The fold derives a mark on the cards that depend on the reopened card. A rejected reopen marks the next hop only as stale, in red. A postponed reopen marks every downstream card as paused, in grey, and keeps the card's status colour. If both reach one card, stale wins. A review of a stale card clears its mark. The staleness view in the command line and in the browser lists each marked card with the reopen reason and the trace that put the mark there.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A reopen with no reason is refused.
- [ ] After a rejected reopen, only the direct dependents are stale, and a card two hops away has no mark.
- [ ] After a postponed reopen, every card downstream at any distance is paused, and the browser draws it grey with its status colour kept.
- [ ] A card reached by both a postponement and an invalidation shows as stale.
- [ ] The staleness listing prints each mark with its reopen reason and its trace, and the browser shows the same reason on the card.
- [ ] Clearing the mark on one stale card leaves the other marks in place.

## What to build

When a ticket ends, green or capped, the loop writes one summary record for it beside the run's pass records. The summary holds: the ticket id, its story count, the number of builder passes, the tokens spent, the wall time, the number of grader fails, the number of review fails, and how it ended.

The numbers come from work the loop already does. No extra agent call collects them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A ticket that goes green on pass 3, after one grader fail and one review fail, writes a summary with 3 passes, 1 grader fail, 1 review fail.
- [ ] Tokens and wall time are the sums over the passes, including grader and reviewer calls.
- [ ] A capped ticket writes a summary that says capped.
- [ ] The story count is the length of the ticket's stories list, read before the build starts.

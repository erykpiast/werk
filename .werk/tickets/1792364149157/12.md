## What to build

The tab strip shows at most five chips. A sixth conversation parks the least recently touched idle tab. A tab with a running turn is never parked.

The strip ends with a '…' menu. It lists every conversation, marks visible, parked, and active ones apart, and its button shows the total count. A click on a visible one switches to it. A click on a parked one un-parks it with a resume from disk and parks whatever is now least recent.

A tab's close control parks it at once; it does not end the session. A parked session's process is killed after 30 minutes idle, and a reopen resumes it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With six conversations, five chips show and the least recently touched idle one is parked.
- [ ] A tab with a running turn stays visible when a new fork would otherwise park it.
- [ ] The '…' menu lists every conversation with visible, parked, and active marks, and the button shows the total count.
- [ ] Un-parking a conversation starts its child with a resume of its stored session id.
- [ ] The close control parks the tab, and its session stays listed in the '…' menu.
- [ ] A parked child idle for longer than the idle limit is killed, and a reopen resumes it. The limit is a parameter, 30 minutes by default.

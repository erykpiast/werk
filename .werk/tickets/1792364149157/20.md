## What to build

When a builder starts on a ticket, a step author agent starts beside it and writes held-out steps from the ticket brief. Each step follows one path through the whole product, the way a user does. A binder agent turns the steps into tests. The builder's worktree, prompt, and checks never contain the held-out steps.

A command runs the held-out set on the run's tree. A red step files a follow-up ticket into ready and gives the builder the text of the failed step. G2 refuses to open until every held-out step is green on the final tree.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The step author starts when the builder starts, and writes steps from the ticket brief.
- [ ] No builder prompt, worktree, or check contains the held-out steps or their tests.
- [ ] A red held-out step files one follow-up ticket in ready, linked to the step.
- [ ] G2 refuses while any held-out step is red on the final tree, and opens when all are green.

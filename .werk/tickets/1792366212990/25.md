## What to build

The board has five columns: blocked, ready, building, merging and done. A ticket moves right as the loop moves it. Agents and the merge loop make every move outside the person's actions, and a red check in merging moves the ticket back to building. The held-out set does not move a ticket. A red held-out step shows as a badge on the done ticket whose merge it followed, on the card and in the drawer metadata, and it links to the follow-up ticket in ready.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] During a fake run, a ticket passes through ready, building, merging and done in the browser.
- [ ] A red check in merging moves the ticket back to building with no person action.
- [ ] A red held-out step after a merge shows a badge on that done ticket and in its drawer, and the badge opens the follow-up ticket.
- [ ] The follow-up ticket appears in ready.

## What to build

A card, an option, a source or an excerpt that an agent adds is a proposal. It is its own event type, not a flag on an added card. The map draws it as a grey, dashed ghost under its proposed parent, distinct from the mark of a pending edit on a real card. The words of a ghost are frozen: a second add, a body, why or title change all refuse. A link, a status and the who field still work. A link that names a ghost is held, drawn on the ghost, and goes live by itself when both ends are real. The contract check at decided reads the held edges too, so a ghost can be proposed already decided, with its basis and options, as one unit.

The person accepts one ghost at a time with a ✓ on the card. The ✓ appends an event. An accept also accepts the ghost refines ancestors up to the first real card, and the ghost options and sources of the card. A discard drops the card and its ghost descendants. A ghost is outside every rollup, staleness mark and unchallenged count. An accept from the command line refuses unless it states that the person asked for it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every add prints "proposed", and the new node is a ghost in the fold.
- [ ] A ghost is drawn grey and dashed under its proposed parent in the browser, and a ✓ click turns it into a real card.
- [ ] Accepting a ghost child whose parent is also a ghost makes both real. Discarding a ghost parent drops its ghost children.
- [ ] A second add with the same id, and a body, why or title change on a ghost, all refuse.
- [ ] A link and a status set on a ghost succeed. Setting a ghost to decided with a missing fact fails the contract check over the held edges.
- [ ] A held edge becomes live with no new event when its last ghost end is accepted.
- [ ] Rollups, staleness marks and the unchallenged count are the same before and after a ghost is added.
- [ ] An accept from the command line without the person's claim exits non-zero and appends nothing.
- [ ] No control accepts more than one ghost at a time.

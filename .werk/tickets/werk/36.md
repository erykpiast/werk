## What to build

The start of a build appends a card obligation to the decision log. A pass closes it by proposing one card per decision that no card gives, or with the followed verb, which records "no new decision" and names the decided cards the pass followed. The followed verb refuses an unknown or closed obligation and any card that is not a decided, real card. The grader fails the pass as an unrecorded decision when a choice in the diff comes from none of the named cards.

The next pass names the cause of the last failure. A pass whose cause is a spec gap, or a grader gap that says a person must settle it, does not count against the cap. Two passes in a row with findings in the same area that no cited card answers also make the next pass a decision pass: the builder states the question, picks the answer that is easiest to undo and safest when wrong, proposes it as a card with its options and depends_on links, cites it from then on, and builds on it. An agent never accepts it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pass that records followed cards closes the obligation, and a second followed call is refused.
- [ ] A followed call naming a ghost card exits 1 and writes nothing.
- [ ] A spec-gap failure on pass 5 leaves the counted passes at 4 and runs a decision pass.
- [ ] The decision pass's proposal is a ghost, and the ticket's later grades judge against it.

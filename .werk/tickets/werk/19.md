## What to build

No view edits a card in place: each control appends an event through the same rules as the command line, and the page redraws from the log.

A ghost is drawn dashed in the tree under its proposed parent with a blue border, a blue background and the badge "proposed". A card with a pending edit has a blue border and shows its changes inside the text: added words highlighted, removed words highlighted and struck, list items paired one by one. The ✓ in the hero's top-right corner accepts one proposal at a time.

The ✕ removes a card after one confirmation that names how many cards go. For a card with children the confirmation offers the whole subtree, the card alone with its children moved up to its parent (with the counts and the parent's name), or cancel. A card with a pending removal is drawn in red with a red ✓.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A ghost shows dashed with the badge "proposed", and its ✓ makes it a normal card with no reload.
- [ ] A pending edit shows one card with its added and removed words marked, and no before and after sections.
- [ ] The ✕ on a card with two children offers both answers with their counts; moving up keeps the children in the tree.
- [ ] A proposed removal shows red, and its ✓ takes the card out.
- [ ] A ✓ request without the token is refused.

## What to build

The followup verb appends one follow-up record for a done ticket to the board log: its class, plan or build; where it was found, at review or after merge; one line of reason; and for a build miss whether a check could have caught it. A build miss needs that answer, and a plan miss refuses it. A correction names the record it corrects, and the last correction is the one in force. A list option prints the ticket's records, numbered, each with the correction in force. A ticket that does not exist or is not done is refused.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A build-miss record without the catchable answer is refused.
- [ ] A record on a ticket in building is refused and appends nothing.
- [ ] A correction of record 1 shows in the list as the class in force.

## What to build

Decision pass: a held-out step of werk/17 contradicts a decided card, so no code can satisfy both. Decide whether the card or the step is right, and propose the answer as a card. If the answer changes what the product does, build it.

Scenario: The first screen shows the requirements and the build rules side by side
Given a map with a #root card and a #how card, each with children
When the person opens werk at its home address
Then the page shows two equal columns
And the left column shows the title, the why, the counts, and the children of #root
And the right column shows the title, the why, the counts, and the children of #how
But when the map has no #how card, the right column shows an empty build-rules card

It contradicts #vi-onview-root: The card says the map opens on the page of #root, with the same shape as every card page: detail on top, the cards that refine #root below, and 'The root view lists nothing else.' The step asks for two equal columns, one for #root and one for #how, with an empty build-rules card when #how is missing. That is a special root page with a second card on it, which the card rules out (#vi-onview also says there is no side panel). No code could satisfy both. The binder's observation, a single #root page, matches the card.
